# Adonis

> Adonis is a healthcare Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) technology company that helps healthcare providers maximize revenue and reduce the cost and time of collections through an AI orchestration platform, real-time patient eligibility and benefits verification, denial prevention, and claims and payment automation. Founded in 2022, headquartered in New York, and backed by General Catalyst.

## APIs

- [Adonis Platform](https://adonis.io/platform): RCM platform including patient eligibility/benefits verification, patient and appointment data retrieval, claims, and payment workflows. API base: https://api.adonis.io (OAuth 2.0 / OIDC via Auth0, auth-gated).

## Authentication

- [Authentication profile](authentication/adonis-authentication.yml): OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect via Auth0 (issuer https://auth.adonis.io/); authorization_code, client_credentials, refresh_token, device_code, and token-exchange grants.
- [OAuth scopes](scopes/adonis-scopes.yml): Standard OIDC/Auth0 identity scopes.
- [OpenID configuration](well-known/adonis-openid-configuration.json)
- [OAuth authorization server metadata](well-known/adonis-oauth-authorization-server.json)
